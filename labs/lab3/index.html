My favorite dish is PIZZA!

Since I was young, the first dish that always came to mind whenever someone asked me what my favorite
dish was pizza. In elementary school, the after school staff would host a pizza and movie night every Friday,
and I think that is where my love first grew. In terms of toppings and style, my favorite has to be pepperoni and
mushroom in the Sicilian style.

This is a list of ingridients that you need to make the pizza:

Dough Bread flour Water Instant yeast Table salt Extra-virgin olive oil
Sauce Crushed tomatoes Extra-virgin olive oil Garlic Salt and black pepper Dried oregano
Cheese Low-moisture mozzarella cheese
Toppings Chopped mushrooms Pepparoni Slices
